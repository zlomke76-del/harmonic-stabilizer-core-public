What it prevents

Harmonic is designed to reduce ordinary operational drift accumulation before it becomes customer-facing or action-bound.

  • Overconfident risky continuation
  • Contradiction normalization
  • Unsafe agent action
  • Missing escalation
  • Consequence-insensitive response behavior
